## 7. Why Özyurt may rank #1 (hypotheses)

1. **Exact-match money document** — title, H1, slug, and first H2 all lock `kağıthane tesisatçı` + `su tesisatçısı`.  
2. **Historical URL equity** — indexed since Dec 2019; refreshed June 2026 (not a new thin page).  
3. **Topical completeness on one URL** — covers the full PAA set Google associates with the seed (kaçak, tıkanıklık, acil, fiyat, mahalle, FAQ).  
4. **Domain-level local footprint** — many district landings + hizmet pages feed internal links / entity “İstanbul tesisatçı ağı.”  
5. **Aggressive relevance density** — keyword repetition + TOC + table = clear aboutness for classic ranking systems (at cost of AI-content smell).  
6. **Possible off-page / Map Pack assist** — user perception as #1 may mix organic + maps; on-page alone does not prove GBP strength.

Homepage is **not** the reason they rank for these seeds.

## 8. Gaps 724 can exploit (without spammy copy)

### Do
- Treat **one primary money URL** for the seed cluster (homepage *or* a clean dedicated landing — not both competing). Prefer strengthening existing Kağıthane-first home **or** a non-bait `/kagithane-tesisatci` that 301s/canonicals cleanly — **no `25-tl` pattern**.  
- Deepen **first-hand Experience**: more dated saha notları, mahalle-specific building stock notes (already started on hub).  
- Keep **soft claims** (no sabit dakika, no %100, yazılı teklif / 6 ay işçilik) as differentiation.  
- Push **GBP reviews + photos + Q&A** — organic content alone won’t beat Map Pack click share.  
- Use hub as **router**, not as rival money title; align hub title/H1 to “bölgeler / mahalle” and let money URL own “tesisatçı.”  
- Expand FAQ **answers** on the money URL (724 already writes citation-ready 100–200 word answers — that is a GEO advantage).  
- Visible `tel:` CTA + WhatsApp + NAP consistency in citations.

### Don’t copy
- `*-25-tl*` or fake price-in-URL bait  
- “Türkiye’nin / yüzbinlerce / 85 araç / %45” unverifiable metrics  
- Self-hosted 5/5 AggregateRating schema  
- Wall-of-text H2 spam that only swaps the service noun  
- Paraphrasing Özyurt’s paragraphs section-by-section

## 10. Top 5 opportunities — 724

1. **Resolve money-URL ambiguity** — homepage already ranks for the phrase in title; hub is secondary; missing `/kagithane-tesisatci`. Pick one primary; avoid cannibalization.  
2. **Map Pack / real reviews** — Özyurt’s on-page 134 “oy” is weak trust vs genuine Google reviews; 724 can win clicks even before organic #1.  
3. **Out-Experience them ethically** — scale dated saha notları, camera findings, site-yönetimi process (hub already shows the pattern).  
4. **Schema + soft-claim moat** — keep Plumber/FAQ/Service accuracy; avoid AggregateRating theater; lean into yazılı teklif / garanti formu as trust.  
5. **Spoke CTR for mid-tail** — when users search `kağıthane su kaçağı` / `tıkanıklık`, 724’s dedicated landings should beat Özyurt’s in-page section; strengthen hub→spoke anchors and homepage problem chooser.
